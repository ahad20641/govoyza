# Partners

Suppliers who do the visa work in secret. Nothing here may reach a public page, email or PDF (CLAUDE.md rule 1).

- `PartnerInvitations`: invite-link join (single use, 7 days).
- `PartnerOnboarding`: KYC uploads (private disk), encrypted bank details.
- `PartnerAgreement`: click-to-sign (name, time, IP, version, sha256 of the text).
- `PartnerApproval`: admin approve / suspend / reactivate.

Screens: the partner Filament panel (`app/Filament/Partner`) on the partner domain, and the admin Partners
resource. Jobs, earnings and performance pages are S1 shells (tasks 1.20, 1.21, 1.27; Phase 3 for scores).
