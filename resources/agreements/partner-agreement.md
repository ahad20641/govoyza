# GoVoyza partner agreement

> **DRAFT PLACEHOLDER. NOT LEGAL TEXT.** This file must be replaced with the supplier agreement approved by
> GoVoyza's lawyer (Phase 0: "Supplier agreement") before any real partner signs it. When it changes, raise
> `govoyza.partner.agreement_version` in `config/govoyza.php` so every partner is asked to sign again.

The final agreement is expected to cover, at least:

1. **Confidentiality.** Customer data and GoVoyza's prices stay confidential.
2. **No direct contact.** The partner does not contact or solicit GoVoyza's customers.
3. **Service levels.** Processing times, status updates and how late jobs are handled.
4. **Data handling.** How applicant documents are stored, used and deleted.
5. **No forged documents.** The partner never produces or "arranges" fake documents of any kind.
6. **Payments.** Pricing, payout schedule and refunds.
